Period Comparison & Prior Period

When you click 🔄 Compare, the terminal overlays a dashed amber baseline showing the contiguous historical period immediately preceding your active time range.

Range Current Window Prior Period Baseline
1W Past 7 days The 7 days immediately before the current week
1M Past 30 days The 30 days immediately before the current month
6M Past 180 days The 180 days immediately before the current 6 months
YTD Jan 1 to today Same elapsed calendar span starting from Jan 1 of previous year
1Y Past 12 months The 12 months prior (e.g. Sep 2024 to Sep 2025)
2Y Past 24 months The 24 months prior (e.g. Sep 2022 to Sep 2024)
3Y Past 36 months Padded from the Sep 30, 2022 public archive start date
ALL Complete dataset Unavailable (preceding data does not exist before Sep 2022)

Reading Interactive Chart Tooltips

Current Date: Sep 5, 26
Total Users: 10,140,000
Prior Period (Sep 5, 25): 9,842,965
Parentheses show equivalent historical date
  • Solid Line (Violet / Cyan): Current observation date and recorded value for your selected timeframe.
  • Dashed Amber Line: Day-for-day historical observation from the prior period. The date in parentheses tells you the exact historical calendar day being compared.
  • Metric Card Chips: Green (▲) and red (▼) chips show percentage change and absolute count change across the entire prior period.
📈

Analytical Indicators & Ratios

  • 7-Day Moving Average (📈 7D MA): Trailing 7-observation arithmetic mean that filters transient weekend fluctuations and API reporting anomalies.
  • Net Change Velocity (📊 Velocity): Daily addition or loss run rate calculated as the net change divided by elapsed days in the period.
  • Engagement Ratio: Percentage of cumulative registered users active within the trailing 30 days (Active Users / Total Users).
